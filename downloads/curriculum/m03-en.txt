Request for AI

Turn these four messages into a table: action / time / note. Put organizer-confirmed arrangements first, then suggestions and questions.

1. Paste the four messages and request into AI to get a table.
2. Look back at organizer message B: the meeting is 09:00; E’s 08:30 is a suggestion. Check that the table keeps them separate.
3. Then send: “Make this three lines I can read before leaving, followed by one question for the organizer.”
4. Check that you can quickly find the meeting time and ticket deadline, then save your preferred version.
