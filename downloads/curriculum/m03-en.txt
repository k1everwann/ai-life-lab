Your first request

For each item, list conclusion, supporting source wording, confirmed / missing / conflict, and selection reason. Distinguish meeting, download deadline, and departure. Do not judge credibility only by date or filename.

Do it yourself: a new situation

Fictional P: approved 10/26, registration limit 20 people. Q: draft 10/29 suggests 30, unapproved. R: contact message 10/28 says 'limit 25' without stating whether it replaces P. List the approved value and unresolved issues.

Known approved value: 20 (P). The 30-person value is a draft. The 25-person message differs from P, but authority and replacement are unclear. Ask the decision owner; do not assert that 25 or 30 is the final valid limit.

Take the challenge

Preserve uncertainty when no current approved version is available.

Fictional V1: valid through 2026/10/20; fee 300 TWD. V2: draft dated 2026/10/28; proposed fee 350 TWD, unapproved. Inquiry date: 2026/10/29.

Can today's fee be confirmed? State the limitations of both documents and the evidence needed.

The current fee cannot be confirmed. V1 expired; V2 is newer but unapproved. Obtain the current approved fee notice, effective dates, and publisher confirmation. Neither 300 nor 350 can be presented as confirmed.
