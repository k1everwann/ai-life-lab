Plans changed: ask AI to update them

When a time, location, or headcount changes, you do not have to rewrite everything.

What you will make

Get a reminder with an updated ticket deadline and the original meeting details.

Meet on 11/21 at 09:00 at Cloudbank Station.

Practice material

Fictional B0; time zone Asia/Taipei: download tickets by 2026/11/19 20:00; meet at Cloudbank Station on 2026/11/21 at 09:00; check-in time not provided.

Request for AI

Organize these departure details into three lines: ticket download, meeting, and check-in. Write “to confirm” for anything not provided.

1. Paste the data and request below into AI to get your three-line reminder.
2. Paste the second-round data and request into the same chat to change the download deadline to 19:00.
3. Compare the versions: download is one hour earlier, while the meeting stays at 09:00.
4. Save the updated version you want to use. You can also ask to turn it into a checklist.

Try a more involved task

Identify the conflict without choosing an unsupported deadline.

Fictional C1: contact A says download by 2026/11/19 19:00. C2: contact B says 18:30 the same day. Neither states which notice is replaced or who has authority. Time zone Asia/Taipei.

List the conflict, common facts, and who should confirm the answer. Do not treat earlier or newer as a confirmed answer.

Shared fact: download is due on 2026/11/19. Conflict: 19:00 versus 18:30, a 30-minute difference; the valid deadline is unconfirmed. Ask the organizer's decision owner which notice is valid and whether it replaces the other. Do not claim 18:30 is officially adopted.
