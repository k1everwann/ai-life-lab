Your first request

Using L1, write a short Chinese notice and an English version for attendees. Distinguish activity time and reply deadline. Preserve price, per-person unit, time zone, and unknown lunch cost. Keep activity and venue names in Chinese without claiming an official translation. Include a fact comparison.

1. Extract six fields that cannot change: activity date/time, venue, per-person fee, reply deadline, lunch limitation, and name status.
2. Submit source and prompt for both versions. Verify each field against the source rather than checking fluency alone.
3. Follow up: 'Make Chinese friendlier and English shorter without removing the deadline, time zone, or unknown lunch cost.' Compare before and after.
4. Save the source, both versions, and comparison. Keep uncertain proper names in the original language and mark them for confirmation.
