Practice material

Fictional notice L1: 雲岸共學 takes place on 2026/11/21 at 09:00 (Asia/Taipei), at 雲樓A室; 300 TWD per person. Reply whether attending by 2026/11/18 18:00 (Asia/Taipei). Lunch costs extra; its price is undecided. Neither activity nor venue has an approved English name.

Request for AI

Write short Chinese and English notices from these details. Make the time, place, price, and reply deadline easy to find. Keep activity and venue names in their original form and keep the extra lunch cost.

1. Paste the activity details and request into AI and read both notices.
2. Then send: “Make the Chinese sound like a message to a friend, and make the English shorter.”
3. Check that the dates and 300 TWD per person did not change, and lunch still costs extra.
4. Save the versions you like. For unfamiliar English, ask: “Explain this sentence in Chinese.”

After trying: compare a possible result

Reply by 2026/11/18 18:00 (Asia/Taipei). Lunch costs extra; the price is unconfirmed. All six fields agree, with no invented official names.

Try a more involved task

Make a two-sentence spoken script while retaining limitations.

Fictional L3: online exchange 2026/12/10 19:00 (Asia/Taipei); register by 12/08 17:00 (Asia/Taipei); 200 TWD per person; access link not yet provided.

Write two sentences each in Chinese and English: activity and price first, then deadline and missing link. Do not invent a URL.

Chinese: 12月10日19:00有線上交流，每人200 TWD。請於12月8日17:00前報名，參加連結待提供；以上日期皆2026年，時間皆Asia/Taipei。 English: The online exchange is on 2026/12/10 at 19:00 (Asia/Taipei), at 200 TWD per person. Register by 2026/12/08 17:00 (Asia/Taipei); the access link has not been provided. Wording can vary; time zone and missing-link status cannot be dropped.
