Request for AI

Write short Chinese and English notices from these details. Make the time, place, price, and reply deadline easy to find. Keep activity and venue names in their original form and keep the extra materials cost.

1. Paste the activity details and request into AI and read both notices.
2. Then send: “Make the Chinese sound like a message to a friend, and make the English shorter.”
3. Check that the dates and 300 TWD per person did not change, and materials still costs extra.
4. Save the versions you like. For unfamiliar English, ask: “Explain this sentence in Chinese.”
