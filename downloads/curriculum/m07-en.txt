See a reference answer

Original six rows: September 2100; October 2350; increase 250; 250÷2100×100≈11.9%. Categories: meals +150, transport +50, services +50. >50 selects meals only, 1 category; >=50 selects 3. Monthly totals do not change. After an October meals refund of -100: October 2250, increase 150, 150÷2100×100≈7.1%; net meals 1250, with all three categories +50.

Check these points

September: TWD 2,100. October: TWD 2,350. Increase: TWD 250.
250 ÷ 2,100 × 100% ≈ 11.9%.
Only Meals increased by more than 50; all three categories increased by at least 50.
