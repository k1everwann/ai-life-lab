Turn an invitation into a to-do

When you receive an invitation or sign-up notice, find the next thing you need to do.

What you will make

Get a dated to-do and a short reminder you can send to a friend.

Example result

Reply by 11/18 18:00 about attending.
Activity: 11/21; meeting location to be announced.

This is a prepared example. Your AI may use different wording.

Follow along

Work in your own AI tool

Practice material

Fictional teaching notice A1
Activity date: 2026/11/21.
Reply whether you will attend by 2026/11/18 18:00 (Asia/Taipei).
The meeting location has not been announced.

Request for AI

Turn this notice into one to-do: what to do and by when. List any details that have not been announced.

1. Open your usual AI tool in another browser tab and sign in. Keep this lesson open so you can return to it.
2. Copy the practice text and request below, paste both into the AI chat, and send them.
3. Read the answer, then send: “Make this one friendly reminder I can send to a friend.”
4. Check that the shorter version keeps the 11/18 18:00 reply deadline. Copy your favorite version to notes or save it in this lesson’s record area.

After trying: compare a possible result

The reply deadline remains 11/18 18:00; the activity is on 11/21. A friendly version: “Remember to reply by 6 pm on 11/18 about joining the 11/21 activity! We’re waiting for the organizer to announce the meeting location.”

Check these parts of your result

The reply deadline is 11/18 18:00, not the activity date of 11/21.
The short version keeps the action and does not invent a meeting location.

Try a more involved task

Make a two-sentence note for a partner, preserving the deadline and unknown.

Rewrite A3 in two sentences. Put the action and deadline in the first; put the activity date, speaking time, and unknown in the second.
