Reply by 11/18 18:00 about attending.

1. Make a first version

Practice material

Fictional teaching notice A1
School puzzle event date: 2026/11/21.
Reply whether you will attend by 2026/11/18 18:00 (Asia/Taipei).
The meeting location has not been announced.

Request for AI

Write three lines: what I need to do, the reply deadline, and what I still need to ask. Mark missing details “to confirm”.

1. Select “Copy material and request” below.
2. Paste into a new AI conversation and send.
3. Return and check the points below. Ask AI to correct any errors.

See a reference answer

Action: form a team for the November 21 campus puzzle event and reply about joining.
Deadline: 2026/11/18, 18:00 (Taipei time).
To confirm: meeting place.

Check these points

The reply deadline is 11/18 18:00, not the activity date of 11/21.
The short version keeps the action and does not invent a meeting location.

2. Change one thing and check

Make it a friendly, short message for teammates. Keep the reply deadline and leave the meeting place as “to confirm”.

The November 18, 18:00 deadline stays. No meeting place is invented.
