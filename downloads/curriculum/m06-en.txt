Notion: turn rough notes into a plan

After a meeting or chat, organize rough notes into actions before putting them in your notes app.

What you will make

Get a reading-group action table with tasks, owners, and deadlines.

Example result

T01 | Xiaohe | Choose three articles by 11/18.
T02 | Me | Confirm the venue by 11/19.

This is a prepared example. Your AI may use different wording.

Practice material

Fictional teaching page: [AI Lab] Reading group start N1
Activity: 2026/11/21 14:00 (Asia/Taipei), 6 people.
T01: Xiaohe to choose three articles by 11/18, incomplete.
T02: confirm venue by 11/19, owner undecided, incomplete.
Budget undecided.

Request for AI

Organize these notes into activity details, an action table, and things to discuss. Use ID, task, owner, deadline, and progress as columns; mark missing details “to confirm.”

1. Paste the notes and request into AI to get a draft plan.
2. Read the task table: choosing articles and confirming the venue should be separate tasks.
3. Then send: “I will take responsibility for T02, confirming the venue. Update only that item.”
4. Copy the plan you like into your notes. Try the optional connection exercise to put it in an actual Notion page.

Optional: put the plan in your own Notion

Use Notion to read this page: [practice page link]. Organize activity details and an action table. Show the draft in chat first without changing the page.

Connect Notion through available Plugins or connected apps in your AI tool. Use the actual permission flow to allow your practice pages, and confirm you can open them yourself.
Insert the source-page link, select the Notion plugin, and send the request. Review the draft against your notes.
To save it to Notion, send a separate request: “Under [results parent link], create Reading group plan and write the complete draft we just reviewed.” This is the step that creates a page.
Open the returned page and check its title, tasks, and location. Without write access, copy the draft to your practice page yourself.

Reading produces a chat draft. An actual new Notion page exists only after you separately request writing and it succeeds.
