- Keep HTML, CSS, and JavaScript dependency-free. GitHub Pages serves the repository root.
- The homepage is the complete 60-minute lesson: four projects, three worked app lessons, asking/refining/checking, a downloadable checklist tool, and continued practice. Do not split learner content and instructor content into different pages or link to instructor.html/next-steps.html from the main lesson. Keep the earlier phone/computer workshops in practice.html as optional material.
- Label fictional exercises, prepared outputs, and reconstructed project demos.
- Public previews are fictional reconstructions, never captures or embeds of private pages. Keep investment examples free of accounts, holdings, strategies, performance, and live trading connections.
