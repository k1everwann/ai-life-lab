Connect an account: Gmail, Google Drive, Notion

Once connected, AI can read the emails, documents, or notes you specify. Connect inside your own AI tool.

  1. In ChatGPT, open Settings → Plugins and search for the service.
  2. Follow the install or connect prompts. Check the account and which data it can access.
  3. Return to your conversation. Use @ or + to select the service, then paste the exercise request.

Availability depends on your plan and workspace. If you cannot find or connect the service, complete the text exercise first and return to connections later.
