Read the explanation

汗まみれ means covered in sweat. まみれ follows nouns such as sweat or mud to mean covered in that substance. に限って, からこそ, and に至って cannot complete 汗（　）になって here. This example practises the expression 汗まみれ.

02 · Connect data · 24 min

Give AI your data to get an answer about your situation

Start with one email, file, or note. Each lesson below includes steps, paste-ready data, a worked result, and a small practice question.

Connecting an account for the first time: four steps
  1. In ChatGPT, open Plugins and find Gmail, Google Drive, or Notion. In another AI tool, look for its app or connection settings.
  2. Open the plugin details and install it. When asked to connect, sign in to the intended account and review the content and actions you are allowing.
  3. Start a new chat, use the appropriate plugin, and specify the teaching email, file link, or note page. First ask AI to describe what it actually read.
  4. Open the cited source to check it. If the source is unavailable, paste the teaching text below; practise the method before troubleshooting account settings.

Connector demonstrations use a prepared teaching account. To repeat them yourself, first send an email containing A to your own teaching Gmail and confirm it is in the inbox; create B and D as Drive files and C as a Notion page, then specify the actual subject, date, or links. In class, you can use “Copy data + this request” below to practise with the text directly.

Setup varies by tool and account.

Gmail: find the next action in one booking email

What to complete：Find the required reply and deadline, then draft a question about check-in time. The result is a draft you can check.

  1. In your own AI tool, find the apps or plugins connection settings. If Gmail is available, choose the account and inspect the permitted scope. If it is unavailable, use the text-pasting alternative below.
  2. Start another AI conversation. Identify the booking email, for example by a sender, subject, and date you have checked. This lesson uses fictional source A only, not your entire inbox.
  3. Paste the prompt below and send it. In the draft, find the reply deadline, check-in date, and available check-in time. Check that these three have not been merged.
  4. Open the original email cited in the answer and check each item. If the source is inaccessible or incomplete, paste the correct text and ask again. The draft has not been sent; sending needs your separate explicit instruction after checking.

Read the source

A | Gmail booking email (fictional)

Starbay Inn: check in on 2026/11/21 and check out on 11/22. Reply with your party size by 11/18 at 18:00. The email does not give the time when check-in is available.

A request to try

Read only my specified booking email A. List tasks and deadlines with sources, then draft a reply asking about check-in time. Mark missing information “to confirm”. Do not send it yet.

Fixed example: email → task → draft reply

This is a prepared teaching example; your actual AI response may differ.

[Fixed example result; not a live AI response]
Task: reply with party size by 11/18 at 18:00. Source A.
Stay dates: check in 11/21; check out 11/22. Source A.
To confirm: when check-in is available. The actual party size is also not given.

Draft reply:

The draft neither invents the party size nor sends a message. Replying with the party size remains a task.

Check against the source

Find 11/18 at 18:00 in A: it is the deadline to reply with party size. 11/21 is the check-in date; the email gives no check-in hour such as 16:00. With real data, also check the sender and email date. An AI citation alone does not establish trustworthiness.

Without a Gmail connection, manually paste one email you may use, with sensitive details removed, together with the prompt. In class, use fictional A above. Pasting text does not connect to or search your inbox.

Your turn to decide：An AI draft says “Check-in is available on 11/21 at 16:00.” What should you do?

Google Drive: compare two itineraries and retain the current version

What to complete：Identify the change in meeting time, distinguish current and old versions, and keep event times separate from preparation deadlines.

  1. Connect an available Google Drive account in your own AI tool and check the account and permitted scope. This lesson reads two specified teaching documents, not your entire drive.
  2. In a new conversation, specify current itinerary B and old version D. State “Use B as current; D is for comparison only.” If the tool cannot read their contents, paste both texts.
  3. Send the prompt and ask for an item-by-item comparison, separating the meeting time from the ticket download deadline. Seeing a filename does not prove the content was read; check that the answer cites the text.
  4. Open B and D to verify times, version labels, and differences. If real version status is unclear, ask the document owner before choosing a version. Do not leave that decision to AI.

Notion: turn existing notes into a draft update

What to complete：Read one specified note and organise known information, tasks, and open questions. Check the draft before deciding to create a page separately.

  1. Connect an available Notion account in your own AI tool. Check the account, workspace, and readable scope, granting access only to pages needed for this task. This lesson uses fictional note C.
  2. In a new conversation, specify travel preparation note C. Ask only for a draft, with no page creation or update. If the note cannot be read, paste its text.
  3. Send the prompt and read the three groups. Check that a stated deadline has not become a claim that the task is finished, and that open questions have not become invented answers.
  4. Check each item against C. If you then want a page created, give a separate instruction naming the parent page, child-page title, and approved content. The tool needs write capability and permission. Do not count the page as created until you receive a result you can open.

Read the source

C | Notion preparation note (fictional)

Pack rain gear and charging cables on the evening of 2026/11/20, finishing by 21:00. Check-in time is still unconfirmed; ask the inn before departure. There is no exact deadline recorded for that enquiry.

A request to try

Using only my specified travel preparation note C, draft three groups: known information, tasks, and open questions. Cite the source. Known information does not mean completed work. Do not create or update pages yet.

Tasks: pack rain gear and charging cables; ask the inn about check-in time before departure. Source C.

[Example action instruction to use only after checking; this page does not execute it]

04 · Build a checklist · 13 min

Turn the list into a tool you can use

Paste one checked task per line to make a checklist with ticks, a count, and reset. This step uses the website’s fixed template; AI helps organize the content.

After bringing in text, remove headings, reminders, and table separators. Keep actions only. Turn unknowns into tasks such as “Ask the inn about check-in time”.

Try these three things before taking it away

  1. Tick two tasks: the count becomes 2. Untick one: it becomes 1.
  2. Use “Reset all”: the ticks and count both return to zero.
  3. Download the HTML and reopen it in a browser. Check your name and tasks are present.

Open the file from your browser’s downloads. You saved the checklist file; this first version does not retain ticks after a refresh.

Save both the process and the finished tool

The text record keeps your sources, request, follow-up, and checked result. The HTML is your checklist tool. Keep both so you can continue next time.

Look up unfamiliar terms
Prompt (your question)
The request you give AI. Start with the information scope and task, then add a requirement after reading the draft.
Source
The original email, document, or note behind an answer. A source ID helps you find the text but does not guarantee accuracy or credibility.
App or plugin connection
A capability that lets an AI tool read or act in another service within granted permissions. Availability varies; connecting alone does not perform a task.
Draft
Suggested content waiting for your review. An email draft has not been sent; a page draft has not been created; listing a task does not complete it.
Rule-based automation
Following a predefined condition, such as turning on at 28°C. This is a different mechanism from AI generating new text.
