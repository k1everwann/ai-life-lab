Japanese N1

The aim was to understand Japanese usage. Now you can answer a question, read an explanation, and add it to review.

Fixed teaching example · Choose the expression that fits the sentence.

選手たちは汗（　）になって練習を続けた。

Read the explanation

汗まみれ means covered in sweat. まみれ follows nouns such as sweat or mud to mean covered in that substance. に限って, からこそ, and に至って cannot complete 汗（　）になって here. This example practises the expression 汗まみれ.

03 · Try it · 15 min

Your turn: my travel preparation checklist

Practise with the three fictional excerpts below; no account connection is needed. Keep this lesson tab open and open an AI tool you can already use in another tab.

Choose your language before typing. Switching languages or closing the page clears unsaved input; copy and save it first.

1In this lesson: copy the data and first question

A | Gmail booking email (fictional)

Starbay Inn: check in on 2026/11/21 and check out on 11/22. Reply with your party size by 11/18 at 18:00. The email does not give the time when check-in is available.

B | Drive itinerary (fictional)

Meet at Cloudshore Station on 2026/11/21 at 09:00. Download your tickets by 11/19 at 20:00. The meeting time is different from the ticket download deadline.

C | Notion preparation note (fictional)

Pack rain gear and charging cables on the evening of 2026/11/20, finishing by 21:00. Check-in time is still unconfirmed; ask the inn before departure. There is no exact deadline recorded for that enquiry.

List the tasks and deadlines before departure, with sources.

2In your AI tool: send it and read the first draft

Switch to your AI tool, paste, and send. Read the draft, find one thing you would improve, and return here.

3Write a follow-up here, then send it to your AI

For example: sort by deadline; or label uncertain details “to confirm”.

Paste and send the follow-up in the same AI conversation. Return here with the revised answer to check it.

4Back here: check sources and keep your result

Check against the original text, then consider whether the source is trustworthy. For real data, also check the sender, document version, and publication date. Ticking a box is your own self-check.

Need a reference? See the fixed example result

This teaching example was written in advance; it is not a live AI response. Without an AI tool, you can practise checking it. Mark your record “Example only”.

• By 11/18 at 18:00: reply with party size. Source A.
• By 11/19 at 20:00: download tickets. Source B.
• By 11/20 at 21:00: pack rain gear and charging cables. Source C.
• Before departure: ask the inn when check-in is available; the exact enquiry deadline is to confirm. Sources A and C.
Reminder: 11/21 at 09:00 is the meeting time (B); the available check-in time is not given (A).
