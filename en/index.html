How to use these lessons

  1. Read the steps here; do the work in your own AI tool.
  2. Try the provided example, then adapt it to your needs.
  3. Save your result and return for another lesson whenever you like.

Bring a computer and an AI tool you can sign in to, such as ChatGPT. Basic exercises do not require Gmail, Drive, or Notion connections.

Choose a lesson

You do not need to finish every lesson or follow this order. Pick a useful task, skip what you know, and explore further when ready.
