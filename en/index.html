Japanese N1

The aim was to understand Japanese usage. Now you can answer a question, read an explanation, and add it to review.

Source data
One teaching sentence with fixed choices, an answer, and an explanation. The question is not generated live.
What AI can help with
AI can help draft questions and explanations, but a teacher or reliable learning material must check the usage and answer.
What the system does
Code compares your choice with a fixed answer and shows the explanation. “Add to review” marks a review item on this page only.
Your first small step
Answer one question, explain your choice, and keep an unfamiliar expression for review.

Fixed teaching example · Choose the expression that fits the sentence.

選手たちは汗（　）になって練習を続けた。

Read the explanation

汗まみれ means covered in sweat. まみれ follows nouns such as sweat or mud to mean covered in that substance. に限って, からこそ, and に至って cannot complete 汗（　）になって here. This example practises the expression 汗まみれ.

Teaching data

  3. Check the original text: B explicitly replaces D; E is newer but unapproved; F is a departure time, not a meeting time.
  4. Ask which items still need organizer confirmation. Save the source comparison and questions.
