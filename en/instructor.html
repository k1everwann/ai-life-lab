AI Life Lab

A simple order for this session

Show four projects, then help everyone make a simple travel preparation list. A 60-minute session using computers.

Before the session

  • Open the teaching website and your chosen AI tool. Use a teaching account with verified permissions for connector demonstrations.
  • Open the four real services yourself and check that their screens are suitable for projection. The fictional interactive examples on this site provide a fallback.
  • Prepare teaching emails in Gmail, files in Drive, and notes in Notion. Confirm their contents are readable, then rehearse a request and a source check.
  • Learners do not need to connect private accounts in class; use the same text supplied on the homepage. Learner account and tool availability still needs a pre-class check.

Walk through the same four steps as learners

  1. Copy the practice data and initial question on the teaching page. Switch to the AI tool, paste, and send. Make it clear which window you are using.
  2. Read the AI draft. Ask learners to add one requirement themselves, such as ordering by deadline or listing uncertainties separately.
  3. Check dates and tasks against the three sources. The booking information does not give a check-in time; an AI guess cannot count as confirmed information.
  4. Return to the teaching page, paste the checked list, copy or download it, then reopen the saved file. Learners without AI can examine the fixed example and do the actual AI request later.

Three things to check before leaving

  • Explain what you organized today.
  • Point to a source for one detail and one item that still needs confirmation.
  • Reopen your saved record.

If something does not work

If a real service will not open, use the fictional interactive example. If a connector is unavailable, paste the same teaching text. Without an AI tool, examine the fixed example and record that the request is still to do. Sending email and updating Notion are separate actions: check the content and destination before demonstrating them.

Content and sources

Project concepts were checked against their documentation; public exercises use newly authored fictional data. This website contains no private service links and calls no AI, email, document, device, or brokerage service. Actual connector capabilities depend on the account and permissions available in class.
